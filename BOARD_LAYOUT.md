# Board Layout Specification

## Format

The prototype board is a landscape rectangle at a 3:2 ratio. A physical target is 30 × 20 inches on a six-panel fold. The board must read from all seats, but its primary orientation places faction tracks along the top and the Battle area along the bottom.

Generated concept art supplies atmosphere only. Final production should overlay crisp vector boxes, icons, rules text, tracks, and connection lines. Do not depend on image-generated lettering.

## Information hierarchy

1. A player must identify all legal spaces for one card icon at a glance.
2. Costs and requirements must appear before rewards in the visual reading order.
3. Battle spaces must share a prominent crossed-swords frame.
4. Riches spaces must have a distinct bonus-Mithril well.
5. Observation posts and their connections must remain visible under placed Scouts and Agents.
6. The Dam must visually connect Fangorn to the three protected critical locations.

## Board zones

### Top band — the four factions

Four equal vertical courts run left to right:

1. **Shadow Hosts** — black iron and ember red; track 0–6; Tribute to the Shadow; Pits of Isengard.
2. **Dwarven Holds** — basalt, copper, and forge gold; track 0–6; Dwarven Caravans; Deep Roads.
3. **Elven Realms** — pale stone, silver, and moonlit blue; track 0–6; Hidden Counsel; Mirror of Galadriel.
4. **Wild Kindreds** — moss, bark, and fern green; track 0–6; Hidden Paths; Ranger Mustering.

Each track shows a Renown marker at 2, the one-time favor at 4, and a detachable Alliance-token well beside 4.

### Left column — White Council

Stack five green-grey institutional spaces:

- Hall of Fire
- Muster the Free Peoples
- Seat on the White Council, with Councilor wells
- Secret Bargain
- Captain of the Host

This column should feel like parchment, carved seats, and concentric council geometry rather than a geographic region.

### Center — map and Strongholds

The central illustrated map contains four Stronghold spaces:

- Minas Tirith, critical and protected
- Archives of Rivendell
- Fangorn Moot
- Osgiliath, critical and protected

Edoras appears along the western edge of this map as a Roads space, critical and protected. Each critical location has a Banner well and an income icon.

The Dam token lies between Fangorn Moot and a three-pronged route toward Minas Tirith, Osgiliath, and Edoras. An intact side shows closed stone and barred Ent silhouettes; the reverse or empty well shows rushing water and open roots.

### Right column — Roads and deep places

Stack four further Roads spaces around the Edoras space:

- Take Up a War Effort
- Great Forge
- Deep Fangorn, with a large bonus-Mithril well
- Entwash, with a bonus-Mithril well

Edoras also receives a bonus-Mithril well, completing the three Riches spaces.

### Bottom center — Battle

The Battle area contains:

- Battle-deck well and active-card well;
- four player quadrants for deployed units;
- a 0–20 Strength track;
- reminder icons for Company `2`, Ent `3`, Ent reward doubling, and tie order;
- three Standard icons: White Tree, Horse, Star.

Four garrison circles sit around the Battle area, one closest to each seat.

### Outer edge

A 0–15 Renown track runs around the lower and side edges. The Riches and Recall reminders sit near the round-order panel. Banks and card rows remain off-board to prevent the map from becoming too dense.

## Observation-post network

Use nine posts. Printed connector lines are rules, not decoration.

| Post | Connected destinations |
| --- | --- |
| Orthanc's Eye | Tribute to the Shadow; Pits of Isengard; Secret Bargain |
| Redhorn Pass | Dwarven Caravans; Deep Roads; Great Forge |
| The Last Homely House | Hidden Counsel; Mirror of Galadriel; Archives of Rivendell |
| Northern Eaves | Hidden Paths; Ranger Mustering; Deep Fangorn |
| Council Antechamber | Hall of Fire; Seat on the White Council; Captain of the Host |
| Muster Field | Muster the Free Peoples; Minas Tirith; Osgiliath |
| Old South Road | Take Up a War Effort; Osgiliath; Edoras |
| Banks of Entwash | Fangorn Moot; Entwash; Deep Fangorn |
| Seeing-stone Road | Mirror of Galadriel; Secret Bargain; Minas Tirith |

Every destination is connected to at least one post. High-demand spaces have one connection unless their strategic identity calls for contestable flexibility. The Archives, Deep Roads, and Captain space are deliberately reachable through only one post.

## Space template

Each destination uses the same internal grammar:

- upper-left: placement icon;
- upper-right: battle and/or Riches tag;
- title strip;
- cost/requirement row in red or dark brown;
- effect row in black on light parchment;
- lower edge: controller income and Banner well, where applicable;
- outside edge: observation-post connection nodes.

Text should be horizontal. Icon-only shorthand is acceptable only when the glossary and player aid use the same icon.

## Card-table layout

Place the five-card Chronicle Row along the long edge nearest the current first player, with the deck at one end and the two Reserve stacks at the other. Put Fate and War Effort decks near their related board spaces. Allow at least 3.5 × 5 inches per player for the Commander, Agents, draw deck, discard pile, Journey row, and Muster row.
