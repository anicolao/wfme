# Prototype Card Catalog

Version 0.1. Numbers are starting hypotheses for paper testing. All wording uses the controlled vocabulary in `RULES.md`.

## 1. Shared starting deck

Every Commander uses the same ten cards. Identical decks isolate leader balance, teach one common opening vocabulary, and ensure that faction access is not determined before the first draw.

There are no leader-specific starting-card replacements in the baseline game. If later tests show that Ring abilities cannot carry enough identity, signature replacements should be introduced as a separately measured variant.

## 4. Commanders

Each Commander has one persistent ability and one Ring ability. Difficulty is an onboarding estimate, not a power rating.

### Aragorn — Heir Returned

**Difficulty:** introductory

**The Line Unbroken:** The first time each round you gain standing with a faction where you already have at least 2 standing, recruit 1 Company.

**Ring — Andúril Aflame:** Gain 1 standing with any faction where you have 1 or less.

### Théoden — King of the Mark

**Difficulty:** introductory

**Forth Eorlingas:** The first time each round you send an Agent to a battle space, recruit 1 Company.

**Ring — Ride Now:** Gain 1 Provision. On this Agent turn, you may deploy one additional Company from your garrison.

### Galadriel — Lady of Light

**Difficulty:** intermediate

**Foresight:** Once per round when you would draw a Fate card, look at the top two instead, take one, and put the other on the bottom of the Fate deck.

**Ring — Mirror Unveiled:** Place 1 Scout, then draw 1 card if you have Scouts on at least two different observation posts.

### Gandalf — Bearer of Narya

**Difficulty:** intermediate

**A Wizard Is Never Late:** Once per round after you acquire a card costing 5 or more Influence, draw 1 card.

**Ring — Kindle Courage:** Choose one: draw 1 Fate; or recruit 2 Companies if you have fewer Companies in your garrison than every opponent.

### Éowyn — Shieldmaiden

**Difficulty:** intermediate

**No Living Man:** Once per Battle, after an opponent's Combat Fate card raises their Strength above yours, gain 2 Strength for that Battle.

**Ring — Choose Deeds:** You may trash one card from your hand or discard pile. If you do, draw 1 card.

### Saruman — Voice of Many Colours

**Difficulty:** advanced

**Engines of Isengard:** The first time each round you recruit at least two Companies in one effect, you may pay 1 Gold to recruit one additional Company.

**Ring — A Fair-seeming Promise:** Gain 1 Gold for each faction where you have at least 2 standing, to a maximum of 3 Gold.

### Witch-king — Lord of the Nine

**Difficulty:** advanced

**Black Breath:** Once per Battle, when you play a Combat Fate card, choose an opponent; that opponent loses 1 Strength for each of your Ents in the Battle, plus 1.

**Ring — Terror Rides:** Recruit 1 Company. If your Agent went to a battle space, deploy that Company immediately and draw 1 Fate.

### Treebeard — Eldest of the Ents

**Difficulty:** advanced

**Roused at Last:** When you first reach Wild standing 2, take an Ent-draught token without visiting Fangorn Moot. The first time each game you summon exactly one Ent, summon one additional Ent.

**Ring — Roots and Stone:** Gain 1 Provision. If the Dam is breached, gain 1 Mithril.

## 9. Prototype content notes

- Card names describe broad fantasy roles and are not intended as quotations from the novels or films.
- The Chronicle deck is intentionally smaller than the reference game's market deck so early testing sees each card often enough to produce useful data.
- Market cards use two copies each to make synergies discoverable. Later versions may introduce single-copy legendary cards.
- “Draw 2 Fate, keep 1” means discard the unchosen card face up.
- Effects granting Strength last only for the active Battle unless explicitly tied to a unit.
