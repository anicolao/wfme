The War for Middle-earth
A Strategy Board Game of Alliances, Combat, and Dominion
1. Overview
The War for Middle-earth is a game of deck-building and worker placement where 1–4 players lead unique Commanders—from Aragorn to the Witch-king—battling for control of the lands. You will dispatch Agents to gather resources, court the great Factions, and maneuver your armies into position.
But influence alone is not enough. At the end of every round, steel meets steel. You must muster Levies, breed elite Uruk-hai, and perhaps even rouse the Ents of Fangorn to crush your enemies in decisive battles.
Objective: The first player to reach 10 Victory Points (VP) triggers the endgame. Points are earned through Combat, Faction Alliances, War Efforts, and hidden Fate Cards.
2. Core Resources & Economics
Managing your logistics is key to survival.
Gold: The currency of the civilized world. Used to pay for Council seats, hire mercenaries, and fund War Efforts.
Mithril: A rare, high-value ore mined from the deep places. It is the fuel of heavy industry and essential for high-level transport via the Deep Roads.
Provisions: Food and supplies. Essential for moving troops into the dangerous "Wild" spaces on the board.
Ent-draught: A mystical substance required to wake and command Ents.
3. The Armies
Military strength is measured in generic power, but unit types determine your tactical ceiling.
A. Levies (Standard Units)
Strength: 1
Description: The gathered masses—human soldiers, orcs, or easterlings. They are expendable and plentiful.
B. Uruk-hai (Elite Units)
Strength: 2
Description: Bred for war. They are harder to acquire but provide superior force concentration.
Acquisition: Primarily recruited through The Shadow faction track.
C. Ents (Siege Units)
Strength: 3
Special Rule: Siege Breakers. If you have an Ent in a conflict, the rewards for winning that conflict are Doubled.
Requirement: You must spend 1 Ent-draught to muster an Ent into battle.
Restriction: Ents generally cannot enter the "Settled Lands" unless The Dam is broken (see Section 6).
4. The Game Board
The board is divided into political factions and geographical regions. To visit a space, you must play a card from your hand with a matching icon.
I. The Four Great Factions
Gaining influence on these tracks grants Victory Points and gameplay bonuses.
The Elven Realms
Theme: Ancient wisdom, foresight, and magic.
Strategic Benefit: This track specializes in Card Draw and Deck Manipulation (filtering weak cards out of your deck).
Key Reward: Elven Wisdom (tokens that allow you to redraw hands).
The Dwarven Holds
Theme: Craftsmanship, economy, and underground travel.
Strategic Benefit: The primary source of Mithril and industrial capacity.
Key Action: The Deep Roads. Spend Mithril to deploy massive reinforcements directly to a conflict zone.
The Shadow
Theme: Industrial might and ruthlessness (Mordor/Isengard).
Strategic Benefit: The most efficient source of Gold and Uruk-hai.
Key Reward: The Black Breath. An Alliance bonus that reduces enemy combat strength.
The Wild
Theme: The untamed nature of Fangorn and the Rangers.
Strategic Benefit: Guerrilla warfare and access to Ents.
Key Reward: Ent-draught. Necessary to activate your siege units.
II. The White Council (Political Center)
These green spaces represent the overarching coordination of the Wise.
Council of Elrond: Spend Gold to gain a permanent seat (High Council), granting +2 Persuasion every round.
Captain of the Guard: Spend Gold to permanently unlock your Third Agent.
Summon Ally: Hire a temporary powerful agent (e.g., a Great Eagle or Nazgûl) for a single round.
III. The Map (Conflict Zones)
The Settled Lands: Cities like Osgiliath and Edoras. Safe terrain, but restricted for Ents.
The Deep Wilds: Dangerous terrain (Moria, Deserts). Requires Provisions to enter but yields rich Mithril rewards.
5. Gameplay Loop
The game is played over a series of rounds. Each round has five phases.
Phase 1: Round Start
Reveal a new Conflict Card. This sets the stakes for the round (e.g., "Battle for Helm's Deep" - Reward: 2 VP).
Phase 2: Player Turns (Agent Placement)
Players take turns playing one card to send an Agent to the board.
Play a Card: Match the icon on the card to the board space.
Pay Costs: Pay any Provisions or Gold required by the space.
Resolve Effects: Gain the benefit of the Board Space AND the benefit of the "Agent Box" on your played card.
Muster Troops: If the space has a crossed-swords icon, you may deploy troops from your garrison to the active Conflict.
Phase 3: Reveal Turn
When you cannot or choose not to play more Agents, you Reveal.
Reveal Hand: Show all remaining cards in your hand.
Calculate Persuasion: Total the blue Persuasion numbers. Buy new cards from the Market to strengthen your deck.
Calculate Swords: Total the sword icons in your revealed hand. Add these to your army's strength in the Conflict.
Phase 4: Combat Resolution
Determine Strength: Sum the strength of your deployed units (Levies + Uruks + Ents) plus any swords from your Reveal turn and Fate Cards.
Declarations: Players may play "Combat" Fate Cards to alter the outcome.
Awards: The highest strength wins the 1st Place reward on the Conflict Card.
Reminder: If you have an Ent, double your rewards (Gold, VP, or Resources).
Phase 5: Makers (Recall)
Recall Agents. If you control certain board spaces (e.g., Mines), they may produce passive income now.
6. Unique Mechanics
The Scouts
You have cylindrical Scout tokens that live on the connections between board spaces.
Observation: Scouts act as a claim on future turns.
Infiltration: If a space is blocked by an opponent, you may recall a Scout adjacent to that space to enter it anyway.
Intel: You may recall a Scout to draw a card.
The Dam & The Breach
A physical barrier (The Dam) separates the Wild from the Settled Lands.
Intact: Ents cannot be deployed to Settled Lands conflicts.
Breached: Once the Dam is broken (via specific "Bomb" cards or Conflict rewards), the floodwaters—and the Ents—can rush into the cities. Ents may now be deployed anywhere.
War Efforts
A side board contains "War Effort" cards (Contracts).
Send Agents to specific spaces to collect these contracts.
Complete their conditions (e.g., "Spend 5 Mithril," "Win a Battle with 3 Uruk-hai") to gain immediate bursts of Gold, Influence, or VPs.
Commanders
Each player chooses a unique Commander board at the start of the game.
XP & Leveling: You gain XP by winning battles or buying expensive cards.
Skill Trees: Spend XP to unlock permanent abilities unique to your character (e.g., Saruman produces Uruk-hai cheaper; Galadriel can foresee the top of the Fate deck).
7. Setup
Board: Unfold the map. Place the 4 Alliance Tokens on the bottom of the Faction Tracks.
Market: Deal 5 cards from the Main Deck to the Market row.
Conflict Deck: Construct the deck with Level I, II, and III conflict cards.
Players: Each player takes a Commander Board, 2 Agents, and a starting deck of 10 cards.
Resources: Each player starts with 1 Provision and 0 Gold/Mithril.
