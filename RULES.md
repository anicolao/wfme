# Rules of Play

Version 0.1 — core rules for 1–4 players

## 1. Objective

You are a Commander contending to shape the fate of Middle-earth. Gain Renown by earning faction standing and alliances, prevailing in Battles, matching Standards, completing card objectives, and revealing Endgame Fate cards.

At the end of a round, the game ends if at least one player has 10 or more Renown or if the Battle deck is empty. The player with the most Renown wins.

## 2. Components

### Shared components

- 1 game board
- 1 Dam token
- 4 Ent-draught tokens
- 4 faction Alliance tokens
- 8 Ent figures or tokens
- Gold, Mithril, and Provision tokens
- 54 Chronicle cards
- 18 Reserve cards: 8 **Muster the Host**, 10 **Deed Worthy of Song**
- 30 Fate cards
- 16 Battle cards: 3 Age I, 9 Age II, 4 Age III
- 4 Objective cards
- 8 Commander boards
- 1 First Player marker
- optional: 12 War Effort cards

Resource tokens and Ents are not intended to be limited. Use substitutes if the bank runs out.

### Per player

- 1 ten-card starting deck
- 3 Agent pawns
- 16 cubes: 12 Companies and 4 faction markers
- 3 Scout cylinders
- 3 Banner discs
- 1 Renown marker
- 1 Councilor token
- 1 combat marker with a `+20` reverse
- 1 garrison area and 1 player supply

## 3. Core terms and symbols

**Gain** means take from the bank. **Pay** means return to the bank. **Recruit** means move a Company cube from your supply to your garrison. **Deploy** means move a unit into the current Battle. **Recall** means return a piece to your Leader or supply as specified. **Trash** means permanently remove a card from your deck to the shared trash.

**Draw** means take the top card of your personal deck into your hand. If the deck is empty, shuffle your discard pile to form a new deck and continue drawing. If both are empty, draw as many as possible.

**May** and **up to** are optional. All other instructions are mandatory if possible. A slash such as “Gold / Mithril” presents a choice. Resolve a card from top to bottom unless it says otherwise.

The seven normal placement icons are **Shadow**, **Dwarven**, **Elven**, **Wild**, **Council**, **Stronghold**, and **Roads**. **Scout** is a conditional eighth icon described in section 10.

## 4. Card anatomy

Every starting, Chronicle, and Reserve card has:

- a purchase cost in its upper-right corner;
- zero or more placement icons;
- a **Journey box**, used only when the card is played for an Agent turn;
- a **Muster box**, used only when the card is revealed during a Reveal turn.

Playing a card for a Journey ignores its Muster box. Revealing a card for Muster ignores its placement icons and Journey box.

Fate cards are separate from your deck and have a timing label: **Plot**, **Combat**, or **Endgame**.

## 5. Setup

1. Place the board centrally. Put the Dam token on its marked position between Fangorn and the three protected critical locations.
2. Place one Alliance token beside space 4 of each faction track.
3. Build the ten-card Battle deck. Shuffle the 4 Age III cards and place all four face down. Shuffle the 9 Age II cards and deal five face down on top. Shuffle the 3 Age I cards and deal one face down on top. Return unused Age I and II cards unseen to the box.
4. Shuffle the Fate deck. Shuffle the Chronicle deck and deal five cards face up as the Chronicle Row. Put the two Reserve stacks face up beside it.
5. Give each player a Commander, color, starting deck, and matching components.
6. Each player puts two Agents on their Commander and the third beside the board. Put three Companies in the garrison and nine in the supply.
7. Each player puts one faction marker at 0 on each faction track, their combat marker at 0, and their Renown marker at 0. In a four-player game, all Renown markers begin at 1.
8. Each player gains 1 Provision. Gold and Mithril begin at 0.
9. Choose the first player randomly and give them the First Player marker. Shuffle the Objective cards and deal one face up to each player. Return unused Objectives to the box.
10. Each player shuffles their starting deck and places it face down beside their Commander. Do not draw yet.

For a first 3–4 player game, use Aragorn, Théoden, Galadriel, and the Witch-king and omit War Efforts. For one or two players, complete setup using `RIVALS.md`.

## 6. Public and private information

Decks, unrevealed Fate cards, and unrevealed Battle cards are hidden. A player may count any deck but may not inspect or reorder it. Hands are private, but each player must state how many cards they hold when asked.

Discard piles, trashed cards, resources, garrisons, supplies, Scouts, Banners, Objectives, acquired Battle cards, faction positions, Ent-draught tokens, and Commander abilities are public. Fate cards in hand are private, but their count is public.

## 7. Round structure

Each round has five phases:

1. Round Start
2. Player Turns
3. Battle
4. Riches
5. Recall

### 7.1 Round Start

Reveal the top Battle card and place it in the active Battle area. If it bears a Minas Tirith, Osgiliath, or Edoras icon, that location is **contested** this round. A player who already controls the contested location may immediately deploy one Company from their supply, not their garrison, to the Battle.

Each player draws five cards. Beginning with the first player, resolve any “start of round” effects in clockwise order.

### 7.2 Player Turns

Beginning with the first player and proceeding clockwise, players take one turn at a time. On your turn, either take an **Agent turn** or, once per round, take your **Reveal turn**.

After taking a Reveal turn, take no more turns this phase. When every player has revealed, proceed to Battle.

### 7.3 Agent turn

To take an Agent turn:

1. Choose one card in your hand with a placement icon that can reach your destination.
2. Choose an Agent on your Commander and an unoccupied matching board space. A Scout may permit a normally illegal placement as described in section 10.
3. Verify requirements and pay the board-space cost before gaining any effect.
4. Place the Agent and play the card face up in your play area.
5. Resolve the board space and the card's Journey box in either order. Resolve each individual effect completely before beginning the other.
6. If this is a battle space, you may deploy units as described in section 11.

One card sends one Agent, even if it shows several icons. A space normally holds only one Agent. You may not place an Agent if you cannot pay all mandatory costs or meet all requirements.

An arrow is an optional conversion: pay the cost on its left once to gain the effect on its right once. Resources gained earlier in the same turn may pay an optional conversion, but never the initial cost to enter the board space.

When visiting a faction space, gain 1 standing with that faction in addition to the printed space effect.

### 7.4 Reveal turn

To Reveal:

1. Place every remaining card in your hand face up in a separate Muster row.
2. Resolve all Muster boxes in any order.
3. Total all Influence shown in those boxes plus bonuses from your Council seat, board spaces, Commander, and effects this turn.
4. Buy any number of Chronicle or Reserve cards, one at a time, paying their Influence costs. Put each acquired card into your discard pile. Refill the Chronicle Row to five immediately after each purchase.
5. Total your Battle Strength as described in section 13 and place your combat marker accordingly.
6. Put every card from both your Journey play area and Muster row into your discard pile.

Influence is not a token and cannot be saved. Unspent Influence disappears when this Reveal turn ends. Purchases and Muster effects may be interleaved, so a Muster effect may occur between two purchases.

## 8. Factions and standing

The four factions are Shadow Hosts, Dwarven Holds, Elven Realms, and Wild Kindreds. Each has a standing track from 0 to 6.

Whenever you gain standing, move your marker up one. Whenever you lose it, move down one, never below 0 or above 6. Resolve threshold Renown and Alliance ownership immediately after either movement. If an Alliance holder loses standing and more than one opponent is now tied above the holder, transfer to the earliest of those tied opponents in player order.

### Standing 2: respect

When you reach 2 standing, gain 1 Renown. If you later fall below 2, lose that Renown. Regain it if you return to 2.

### Standing 4: favor and Alliance

Each time you cross from 3 to 4, gain the faction's favor:

- Shadow Hosts: recruit 2 Companies.
- Dwarven Holds: gain 2 Provisions.
- Elven Realms: draw 2 Fate cards, keep 1, and discard the other.
- Wild Kindreds: gain 1 Provision and recruit 1 Company.

The first player to reach 4 also takes that faction's Alliance token and gains its 1 Renown. If another player later has strictly more standing than the current holder, transfer the Alliance and its Renown immediately. A tie does not transfer it. If multiple effects move players, resolve them completely in turn order before checking the next effect.

## 9. Board spaces

An Agent gains the printed effect below. Faction spaces also grant 1 standing as stated above.

### Shadow Hosts

**Tribute to the Shadow** — Gain Shadow standing. If using War Efforts, take one face-up War Effort; otherwise gain 2 Gold.

**Pits of Isengard** — Cost: 4 Mithril. Gain Shadow standing, draw 1 Fate, and recruit 4 Companies.

### Dwarven Holds

**Dwarven Caravans** — Gain Dwarven standing and 1 Provision.

**Deep Roads** — Battle space. Cost: 5 Mithril. Gain Dwarven standing and recruit 5 Companies.

### Elven Realms

**Hidden Counsel** — Gain Elven standing and draw 1 Fate. Then every opponent with at least four Fate cards gives you one random Fate card from their hand.

**Mirror of Galadriel** — Cost: 1 Mithril. Gain Elven standing, draw 1 card, and place 1 Scout.

### Wild Kindreds

**Hidden Paths** — Battle space. Gain Wild standing and draw 1 card.

**Ranger Mustering** — Battle space. Cost: 1 Provision. Gain Wild standing, recruit 1 Company, then you may trash one card from your hand or discard pile.

### White Council

**Hall of Fire** — Draw 1 Fate. During your Reveal turn this round, gain 1 Influence if your Agent remains here.

**Muster the Free Peoples** — Recruit 2 Companies. You may pay 2 Gold to gain 1 Provision.

**Seat on the White Council** — Cost: 5 Gold. The first time you visit, put your Councilor token in an empty seat. For the rest of the game, gain 2 Influence on every Reveal turn. On later visits, instead gain 2 Mithril, draw 1 Fate, and recruit 3 Companies. Seats are not limited; use a nearby area if printed seats fill.

**Secret Bargain** — Requirement: Shadow standing 2. Cost: 3 Gold. You may discard 1 Fate to draw 1 Fate. Recall one of your other Agents from the board to your Commander, then draw 1 card. The recalled Agent may be used again this round. You may not choose the Agent placed on Secret Bargain.

**Captain of the Host** — Cost: 8 Gold if no player has unlocked a Captain; otherwise 6 Gold. You may visit this space only once per game. Put your third Agent on your Commander. It is available beginning with your next turn this round.

### Strongholds

**Minas Tirith** — Battle space. Recruit 1 Company and draw 1 card. Its controller gains 1 Gold.

**Archives of Rivendell** — Battle space. Cost: 2 Provisions. Recruit 2 Companies and draw 2 cards.

**Fangorn Moot** — Battle space. Requirement: Wild standing 2. Choose one:

- If you do not have an Ent-draught token, take one; then recruit 1 Company and gain 1 Provision.
- Gain 1 Provision; then you may breach the Dam.

If you already have an Ent-draught token, you may not choose the first option.

**Osgiliath** — Battle space. Choose a cost of 0 or 1 Mithril. Gain 2 Gold if you paid 0, or 4 Gold if you paid 1. Its controller gains 1 Gold.

### Roads

**Take Up a War Effort** — Draw 1 card. If using War Efforts, take one face-up War Effort; otherwise gain 2 Gold.

**Great Forge** — Requirement: Dwarven standing 2. Cost: 3 Mithril. Gain 5 Gold, then gain 1 standing with any one faction.

**Deep Fangorn** — Battle and Riches space. Cost: 3 Provisions. Take all bonus Mithril here. Then choose either gain 4 Mithril, or, if you have an Ent-draught token, summon 2 Ents.

**Entwash** — Battle and Riches space. Cost: 1 Provision. Take all bonus Mithril here. Then choose either gain 2 Mithril, or, if you have an Ent-draught token, summon 1 Ent.

**Edoras** — Battle and Riches space. Gain 1 Mithril plus all bonus Mithril here. Its controller gains 1 Mithril. The Mithril represents tribute and craft from the Glittering Caves and White Mountains.

## 10. Scouts

Each observation post on the board is connected by printed lines to one or more board spaces. One Scout occupies one post; a post may hold only one Scout total.

### Place a Scout

When an effect says to place a Scout, move one from your supply to any empty observation post. If all your Scouts are on the board, you may first recall one of your Scouts for no benefit. If every post is occupied and you do not free one, the placement is lost.

### Recall a Scout

A recall effect returns one of your Scouts from any observation post to your supply. In addition, you may recall a Scout without a card effect for exactly one of these benefits:

- **Infiltrate:** While choosing a destination, recall your Scout from a post connected to a space occupied by another player's Agent. You may place your Agent there. Your card must still have a matching normal placement icon or qualify through the Scout icon.
- **Gather intelligence:** Immediately after placing an Agent, before resolving the board space or Journey box, recall your Scout from a connected post to draw 1 card.

One Scout cannot provide both benefits. You may recall two different Scouts on one turn, one to infiltrate and one to gather intelligence.

When **Messenger Moth** tells you to place a Scout and then optionally recall a different Scout to draw 1 card, finish the placement first. The Scout just placed is not eligible for that recall. If you recall another Scout, return it to supply before drawing; if no other Scout is on the board, omit the optional effect.

When **Elven Foresight** looks at the top three cards of your deck, only you may see their identities. Choose one exact top-to-bottom permutation and return those same physical cards in that order. This does not draw a card. If fewer than three cards remain in your deck, look at all that remain; with fewer than two, their order is unchanged. Looking does not cause a discard reshuffle.

### Scout placement icon

A card showing the Scout placement icon may send an Agent to any space connected to an observation post holding your Scout. This does not recall the Scout. Occupancy still applies unless another Scout is recalled to infiltrate.

## 11. Units, garrison, and deployment

There are two units: Companies and Ents.

- A Company is worth 2 Strength. Recruited Companies enter your garrison.
- An Ent is worth 3 Strength. Summoned Ents enter the active Battle immediately and never enter a garrison.

When you visit a battle space, you may deploy:

- any number of Companies recruited during that Agent turn, including by both the space and Journey box; and
- up to two Companies already in your garrison before that turn.

You may deploy fewer or none. Companies recruited during earlier turns are no longer “recruited this turn” and count against the two-Company garrison limit.

When an effect summons an Ent, you must have an Ent-draught token and must immediately deploy it. You may not summon an Ent to a protected Battle while the Dam is intact. If an illegal summoning is one option of a board space, choose its other option.

## 12. Critical locations, Banners, and the Dam

Minas Tirith, Osgiliath, and Edoras are critical locations.

Some Battle cards award control of a critical location. When you gain control, place one of your Banners on that location, returning a previous controller's Banner to their supply. Control is not doubled by Ents.

Whenever any player sends an Agent to a controlled critical location, its controller gains the printed bonus after the entering cost is paid but before other effects: 1 Gold at Minas Tirith or Osgiliath, and 1 Mithril at Edoras.

If the newly revealed Battle names a location you control, deploy one Company from your supply as described in Round Start.

The Dam begins intact. While intact, Ents cannot be summoned into Battles for Minas Tirith, Osgiliath, or Edoras. When an effect breaches the Dam, remove its token permanently. The breach has no effect on Ents already in a legal Battle and does not itself summon an Ent.

## 13. Battle Strength

You participate in the Battle only if you have at least one unit there.

During your Reveal turn, total:

- 2 Strength per Company in the Battle;
- 3 Strength per Ent in the Battle;
- 1 Strength per sword in the Muster boxes you revealed;
- any other explicit Battle bonuses.

If you have no units in the Battle, your Strength is 0 even if you revealed swords. Update your combat marker whenever Strength changes. Use its `+20` side for totals above 20.

## 14. Battle phase

### Combat Fate window

Beginning with the first player and moving clockwise, each participating player may play any number of Combat Fate cards or pass. Continue around the table until all participating players pass consecutively. A player who passed earlier may play when the opportunity returns. Resolve each Fate card fully before the next player acts.

### Ranked rewards

Rank participating players by final Strength.

- Highest Strength takes the first-place reward.
- Second-highest takes the second-place reward.
- In a four-player game, third-highest takes the third-place reward.
- A player at 0 Strength takes no reward.

In a three-player game, the third-place reward is used only for a tie for second.

### Ties

If players tie for first, each tied player gains the second-place reward. Nobody gains the first-place reward, wins the Battle, or takes the Battle card. In a three-player game no other reward is given. In a four-player game, if exactly two players tied for first, remaining players compete for third; if three or more tied, no other reward is given.

If players tie for second, each tied player gains the third-place reward and no lower reward is given. If players tie for third, they gain nothing.

### Winning and Standards

The sole first-place player wins the Battle and takes its card face up. After all rewards are resolved, check that card's Standard—White Tree, Horse, or Star. If you own another face-up Battle or Objective with the same Standard, choose one matching pair, turn both cards face down, and gain 1 Renown. A newly won card can make only one pair at that time.

Face-down cards retain no Standard but remain owned. A Battle card's printed Renown reward, if any, is separate from its Standard.

### Ent reward doubling

If you have at least one Ent in the Battle when rewards are taken, gain your ranked reward twice. Multiple Ents do not multiply it further.

Resources, Fate draws, faction standing, Companies, and printed Renown in that ranked reward may be doubled. A choice may be made differently on its second resolution. If a reward offers “pay X to gain Y,” you may pay and resolve it once on each copy.

Do not double:

- control of a location;
- the Battle card itself;
- its Standard;
- a Standard-pair Renown;
- a trigger that happens because you gained something;
- text explicitly marked “once.”

If an Ent is removed before rewards, it does not double them. One Ent is enough even if you did not win.

### Cleanup

After rewards and Standards:

- return all Companies in the Battle to their owners' supplies, not garrisons;
- return all Ents to the bank;
- set combat markers to 0;
- leave the resolved Battle card with its winner, or discard it face up if nobody won.

## 15. Riches phase

Check Deep Fangorn, Entwash, and Edoras. For each that has no Agent, add 1 bonus Mithril from the bank to its bonus area. If it has an Agent, add nothing. Bonus Mithril remains and accumulates across rounds until taken by a visitor.

## 16. Recall phase and endgame

First check whether any player has at least 10 Renown or whether the Battle deck is empty.

If neither is true:

1. Return every Agent from the board to its Commander's Leader.
2. Pass the First Player marker clockwise.
3. Begin the next round.

If either is true, proceed to Endgame:

1. Beginning with the first player, each player may play any number of Endgame Fate cards, resolving them in clockwise order. Continue until all players pass consecutively.
2. Apply all conditional Renown and losses.
3. The player with the most Renown wins.

Break ties in this order:

1. most Mithril;
2. most Gold;
3. most Provisions;
4. most total faction standing;
5. shared victory.

Record the final standings, winning condition, seed, and every tiebreak in the room's finished-match history. The same seated players may then ready for a rematch. When every player is ready, begin a fresh setup with a new deterministic seed: reset all cards, pieces, resources, standing, Alliances, Battles, and round state, while retaining the immutable record of earlier matches in that room.

## 17. Fate cards

Keep Fate cards facedown in a hand separate from deck cards.

- Play a **Plot** only during your own Agent or Reveal turn, before that turn ends.
- Play a **Combat** only during the Combat Fate window while you participate in the Battle, unless the card explicitly permits otherwise.
- Play an **Endgame** only after the game-ending condition is met.

After resolving a Fate card, discard it face up. If the Fate deck empties, shuffle its discard pile to form a new deck.

When told to give a random Fate card, hold the eligible cards face down and let the receiving player select one without seeing their faces.

## 18. Deck-building

The Chronicle Row always contains five face-up cards. When a card leaves it, immediately refill the empty position from the Chronicle deck. If the Chronicle deck is empty, do not refill it; the game does not end.

Acquired cards go to your discard pile unless an effect says otherwise. Reserve cards also go to your discard pile. If a Reserve stack is empty, that card is unavailable.

Resolve a card's “when acquired” effect immediately, before refilling the Chronicle Row or making another purchase.

Trash is public and permanent. You may trash a card only from the location an effect permits. If an unqualified effect says “trash a card,” choose from your hand or discard pile, never your draw deck or a card already played this turn.

## 19. Commander abilities

Each Commander has:

- one persistent ability usable at its printed timing; and
- one **Ring ability**.

When you play **Token of Command** on an Agent turn, resolve your Commander's Ring ability as part of that card's Journey box. You may resolve the board space before or after the ability. Playing Token of Command during Reveal does not trigger it.

Unless stated otherwise, each “once per round” ability refreshes at Round Start. A Commander rule overrides this rulebook only where the two directly conflict.

For **Engines of Isengard**, a single effect qualifies only if it actually moves at least two Companies from Saruman's supply to his garrison. Separate recruitment effects in the same turn are not combined. Resolve any choice already opened by that effect before offering Engines of Isengard. Its first qualifying recruitment spends the once-per-round opportunity even if Saruman cannot pay, has no Company remaining, or declines. If he pays 1 Gold, move one additional Company from his supply to his garrison, then continue the interrupted Agent or Battle resolution. A doubled Ent Battle reward is one doubled effect for this purpose. The ability refreshes at Recall for the next round.

For **Black Breath**, only a Combat-timing Fate card played by the Witch-king qualifies. Resolve that card and any choice it opens first, then the Witch-king must choose one other current Battle participant. That opponent loses 1 Strength plus 1 for each of the Witch-king's Ents currently in the Battle, to a minimum total Strength of 0. The first qualifying Fate spends Black Breath for that Battle; Battle cleanup refreshes it for the next Battle.

For **Terror Rides**, move one Company from the Witch-king's supply to his garrison when the Ring ability resolves. If Token of Command entered a Battle space, immediately move that newly recruited Company from the garrison to the active Battle; this mandatory movement is separate from and does not reduce the ordinary Battle deployment that follows the destination. Then draw one private Fate, even if no Company remained in supply to recruit. At a non-Battle space, the recruited Company remains in the garrison and no Fate is drawn. Resolve all three parts at the Ring's chosen point relative to the destination; when the destination resolves first, finish its ordered choices, then Terror Rides, then ordinary Battle deployment. If the Fate deck is empty, draw no card.

## 20. Optional War Effort module

During setup, shuffle the 12 War Efforts, reveal two beside the board, and keep the rest facedown. When an effect tells you to take a War Effort, take one face-up card and immediately refill its slot. If no card is available, gain 2 Gold instead.

You may hold only one incomplete War Effort. If instructed to take another, either leave it or discard your current one without reward and take the new one.

Each War Effort states a completion timing, condition, optional payment, and reward. When you meet it, reveal the card, pay any listed cost, resolve its reward once, and discard it. A completed War Effort is not part of your deck. Ent doubling never doubles a War Effort reward unless that reward is printed directly as part of a ranked Battle reward.

## 21. Timing and rules precedence

When simultaneous effects controlled by different players occur, resolve them in turn order starting with the first player. When one player controls simultaneous effects, that player chooses their order.

“Cannot” overrides “may” and “can.” Card text overrides the general rule only for the specific effect and duration stated. Costs cannot be reduced below 0, and Battle Strength cannot fall below 0. A player may not choose an option they cannot fully resolve if another legal option exists; otherwise resolve as much of a mandatory effect as possible.

Gaining the same named permanent token twice has no effect. There is no hidden memory: if an effect matters later, mark it with a component or leave its card visible.

## 22. Solo and two-player games

Use the automated-opponent rules and action-card catalog in `RIVALS.md`. Unless that document changes a rule, every rule in this document still applies.

## 23. Learning-game exclusions

Version 0.1 deliberately does not define six-player teams, Commander experience, an elite Uruk-hai unit class, or campaign progression. These require their own tested modules rather than informal rulings.
