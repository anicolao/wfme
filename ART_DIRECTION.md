# Art Direction

## Visual thesis

The world should feel old, material, and contested: inked cartography laid over worn vellum, luminous landscapes framed by carved stone and hammered metal, and figures defined by heraldry rather than celebrity likeness. The tone is grave but not monochrome. Hope appears as cool silver, morning gold, and living green against iron, ash, and storm.

## Guardrails

- Use original high-fantasy interpretations, not film actors, costumes, logos, or published board-game compositions.
- Avoid direct imitation of any named living artist.
- Keep generated art free of required small text; final typography is a separate design layer.
- Do not use decorative pseudo-writing where rules text will later sit.
- Keep faction colors consistent across the board, cards, and player aids.
- Reserve pure white for icons and highlights so accessibility overlays remain legible.

## Generated asset set

### Board key art

A wide, top-down illustrated map with the White Mountains and settled lands at center, shadowed volcanic country in the east, moonlit elven woodland in the north, Dwarven gates in the mountains, and ancient forest in the west. The artwork must leave calm negative space for gameplay overlays.

### Board layout concept

A bird's-eye tabletop-board mockup showing the intended zoning: four faction courts across the top, White Council on the left, map destinations in the center, Roads on the right, and Battle arena below. Generated labels are only compositional placeholders.

### Chronicle card-art sheet

Nine portrait illustrations in a coherent frame-free contact sheet: rider, guide, elven seer, Dwarven smith, northern ranger, Gondorian captain, Uruk-hai captain, great eagle, and grey wanderer. Each vignette needs a clear focal subject and room for later card UI.

### Starting-deck card-art sheet

Seven portrait illustrations corresponding to Rallying Words, Armed Escort, The Open Road, Diplomatic Mission, Reconnaissance, Seek Allies, and Token of Command. These should look modest and practical compared with Chronicle cards.

### Commander portrait sheet

Eight original painted interpretations: returning heir, horse-lord king, elven lady, grey wizard, shieldmaiden, many-coloured fallen wizard, spectral crowned warlord, and ancient tree shepherd. Avoid actor likenesses and recognizable film wardrobe.

## Production note

The current PNGs are concept references. A future print/digital pipeline should crop individual vignettes, repaint artifacts, establish bleed, and add vector frames and typography. No rules-critical information should be baked into raster art.
